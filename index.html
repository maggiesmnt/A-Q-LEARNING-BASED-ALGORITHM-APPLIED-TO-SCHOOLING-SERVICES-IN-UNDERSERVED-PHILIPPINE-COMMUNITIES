Road Accessibility & Hazards

Every road has an accessibility score A from 0 to 1. Weather, local risks, reports, and official advisories update this score for routing.

Active Road Alerts

How the Accessibility Score (A) Is Calculated

A = surface × weather × local risk × reports, limited to 0–1. The same score is supplied to both algorithms.
InputSourceEffect on Accessibility
Surface baselineProvisional road type — concrete, gravel, mountain path, creek/river crossingSets the ceiling: 1.00 / 0.85 / 0.65 / 0.50
Weather feedSimulated rainfall scenario for QA until an official feed is integratedApplies the same rainfall scenario to every algorithm run
Local-risk profileBarangay Laiban SK stakeholder information plus the Step 3 road graphApplies edge-specific penalties/closures for landslide, creek, river, mountain-path, and boat-sensitive routes
External road alertOfficial or partner source: landslide, flooding, washout, fallen tree, impassablePulls A toward the severity floor, weighted by confidence
Official advisoryLGU / DPWH / MDRRMO closure noticeHard override: A = 0 until lifted
Confidence & decay. A single unverified external alert starts at 0.45 confidence; each corroboration adds 0.2; an official advisory is 1.0. Confidence halves every 6 hours, so a segment re-opens on its own once no external source re-confirms the hazard.

How Accessibility Affects Routing

A ScoreRoad StatusRouting Behavior
A ≥ 0.75OPENFully available with normal travel cost.
0.45–0.74CAUTIONPassable but slower. The planner applies a moderate travel-time penalty.
0.20–0.44RESTRICTEDAvailable only with approval and a high travel-time penalty.
A < 0.20CLOSEDRemoved from available actions. Communities with no other route are deferred.
Because MODQL includes A in its state, it can distinguish the same location under different road conditions. See Research & Analysis → Compare Algorithms → SOP 3.
